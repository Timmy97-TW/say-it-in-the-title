The verdict總評

Do one thing first: give the three Plant Assay slides a claim instead of a label, and split the agar slide, because that slide alone is carrying three separate experiments and the single most important number in the talk.

先做一件事：把三張 Plant Assay 投影片的標題從標籤改成主張，並且把 agar 那張拆開，因為那一張同時扛著三個獨立實驗，以及整場演說裡最重要的一個數字。

The science is there and the deck is visually competent: the typography is consistent, the bioreactor slide and the biosafety slide are genuinely well built, and the circuit slide has the best title in the deck. But five of twenty-one titles are full sentences, and all five sit on slides that carry no data. Three slides are doing the work of nine, so the results arrive as a wall rather than as a sequence of claims. And the numbers do not agree: the agar chart shows a rise from 1.00 to 1.41, the arrow next to it says +53%, and the speaker says 35%, all inside the same thirty seconds. A judge who is reading the slide while listening will catch that, and once they catch one number they start checking all of them.

科學內容是有的，投影片的視覺水準也在線上：字體一致，生物反應器那張和生物安全那張做得確實好，基因迴路那張的標題是全場最好的一句。但二十一個標題裡只有五個是完整句子，而這五張全都沒有數據。三張投影片扛著九張的工作量，於是結果是一面牆，不是一串主張。而且數字對不起來：agar 長條圖從 1.00 升到 1.41，旁邊的箭頭寫 +53%，講者口中說 35%，三個數字出現在同一個三十秒裡。評審一邊聽一邊讀，一定會抓到；抓到一個之後，他會開始查全部。

Fix the numbers, put one claim in each results title, and this becomes a strong ten minutes. Leave them, and the team spends its Q&A defending arithmetic instead of talking about the work.

把數字修好、每張結果投影片的標題只放一個主張，這十分鐘就會很有力。不修，團隊的問答時間就會耗在替算術辯護，而不是談自己的研究。
