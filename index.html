Say it in the title.把結論寫進標題。

A slide title is not a label for what is on the slide. It is the one sentence the slide proves. Write that sentence and the audience gets your result even if they look up for three seconds and then look away. 投影片標題不是內容的分類標籤，而是這張投影片要證明的那一句話。把那句話寫出來，觀眾即使只抬頭看三秒，也能帶走你的結果。

“What is the message of this slide?”「這張投影片想說的是什麼？」

Answer that out loud, in a full sentence with a verb in it, and you have written your title. If you cannot answer it, you have found a slide that should not be in the talk. 把答案說出來，寫成一個有動詞的完整句子，標題就完成了。如果答不出來，那就代表這張投影片不該留在這場演講裡。

Written for a ten-minute live talk為十分鐘的舞台演講而寫

On a conference stage the audience turns over. Someone walks in at minute six, looks at your gel, and has no idea what the lanes are or why they should care. Every slide has to re-establish four things on its own: what system you are working in, what you changed, what you measured, and what it means. The title carries the last one. 在會場舞台上，觀眾是流動的。有人第六分鐘才走進來，看著你的膠片，不知道每一道是什麼，也不知道為什麼重要。每張投影片都必須自己交代四件事：在什麼系統裡、改變了什麼、測了什麼、代表什麼意思。最後一件，就由標題來承擔。
